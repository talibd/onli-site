Course Modules:
Module 01: Introduction to AI Foundations

Learn what AI is, how tools like ChatGPT work in simple terms, and why AI skills matter in every profession.

  • What AI is, in plain language

  • How large language models work

  • Exploring everyday AI tools

  • Building confidence with AI

Module 02: Getting Started with AI Assistants

Discover how to set up and use AI assistants like ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini for daily work.

  • Setting up free AI accounts

  • Your first useful conversations

  • Uploading files and images

  • Choosing between different AI tools

Module 03: Prompting Basics & Better Answers

Learn how to write clear prompts that give you useful, accurate and well-formatted answers.

  • Giving context, role and goal

  • Asking for specific output formats

  • Following up and refining answers

  • Saving prompts you reuse often

Module 04: Research & Fact-Checking with AI

Understand how to research with AI and check its answers so you can trust what you use.

  • Researching topics with AI tools

  • Understanding AI hallucinations

  • Checking facts against sources

  • Summarising long documents quickly

Module 05: AI for Everyday Work Tasks

Explore practical ways to use AI for emails, meetings, reports and planning at work.

  • Drafting emails and messages

  • Meeting notes and action items

  • Creating reports and presentations

  • Planning and organising your week

Module 06: Images, Voice & Other AI Media

Learn the basics of creating images, audio and simple visuals with AI for presentations and social posts.

  • Basics of AI image tools

  • Creating simple visuals with AI

  • AI voice, audio and presentation tools

  • Where AI media still falls short

Module 07: Privacy, Safety & AI Ethics

Understand how to use AI safely and protect personal and company information while you use it.

  • What not to share with AI

  • Spotting deepfakes and AI scams

  • Privacy settings in AI tools

  • Bias, fairness and responsible use

Module 08: Problem-Solving & Thinking with AI

Develop the habit of using AI as a thinking partner to break down and solve real problems.

  • Breaking problems into steps

  • Brainstorming options with AI

  • Weighing pros and cons with AI help

  • Keeping your own judgement in charge

Module 09: AI Agents, Automation & What Is Next

Explore where AI is heading, from agents to automation, and what it means for your work.

  • Introduction to AI agents and assistants

  • Understanding no-code automation

  • How AI is changing different jobs

  • Keeping up without the hype

Module 10: Final Practical AI Project

Apply your knowledge through a practical project where you use AI to research, write and solve a real task from your own work.

  • Picking a real task from your work

  • Using AI to research and draft

  • Reviewing and improving the output

  • Final project demo and trainer feedback
