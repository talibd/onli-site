Useful Resources for Learning AI From Scratch

May 9, 2026

5 min read

AI Learning Tips

AI Learning Tips

Start With Free Official Guides

Most AI tools, including ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Canva, publish free help pages and prompting guides. They are a reliable place to learn the basics and understand what each tool can do.

Find Resources For Your Use Case

Look for material that matches what you want to do, such as AI for marketing, automation or visuals. Focused resources are easier to follow than general AI news.

Learn Through Structured Live Sessions

Free content is scattered. Structured sessions led by trainers who have built real AI use cases, like Onli's live webinars, give you a clear order to learn in and a place to ask questions.

  • Gives you a clear learning path

  • Saves time sorting through content

  • Keeps your practice regular

  • Supports long-term growth


Learn A Little Bit Every Day

Rather than saving dozens of tutorials, go through one resource at a time and try it out the same day. Regular short sessions help AI concepts stick far better.

Practise AI On Real Work Examples

Use your own emails, reports and projects as practice material. Working on real tasks shows you what AI handles well and where you need to step in.

  • Makes concepts easier to grasp

  • Builds confidence through practice

  • Keeps your learning hands-on

  • Turns knowledge into skill

Build AI Skills That Grow With You

Good resources plus regular practice give you skills that stay useful as tools change. Keep learning steadily and AI becomes a normal part of how you work.
